A seminar on the conceptual and methodological challenges involved in measuring inequality and discrimination across the social sciences.

[Download syllabus](/files/syllabus-discrimination-inequality-20261025.pdf) (updated 25-10-2026)

## Description

Measuring inequality and discrimination is a difficult endeavour in which each methodological choice is also an ethically loaded choice. We will read approaches from economics and sociology, as well as recent texts from philosophers who have entered the debate. The goal is to learn to read social-scientific studies with a critical eye, and to gain a sophisticated understanding of the methodological, conceptual, and ethical problems associated with them.

## Requirements

1. **Prepare for the seminars.** Read the assigned readings and come with at least one question about them.
2. **Give a presentation** (up to 15 minutes, 30% of the final grade) at the student colloquium in week 12 (11 January) and possibly week 13. Let me know your preferred topic by **30 November**.
3. **Write a term paper** based on your presentation, recommended length around 4000 words. See the [grading criteria](https://dailynous.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/lewin-philosophy-paper-grading-rubric-1-xl-page.pdf). You may submit an initial version for (ungraded) feedback by **22 February 2027**. The deadline is **22 March 2027**.
